how to make stickers

in a very cheap, easy way cuz we ain't paying for sticker printer

what you need

  • clear tape
  • scissors
  • paper with designs either drawn or printed on it
  • parchment/baking paper

steps

  1. cut out your design
  2. stick a tape on the parchment/baking paper, enough to fit the designs you have
  3. on a separate piece of tape, place the design you cut out on step 1, the side with design drawn/printed facing down on the sticky side of the tape
  4. sandwich the design with step 2, sticky side down
  5. cut the design out, leave a margin (this will make your peel off process much easier)
  6. peel off the parchment paper whenever you want to use them

tips!

  • use a ruler or a straight edge and squeegee the sticker make the surface nice and smooth
  • depends on which tape you use but try not to apply on uneven surfaces, or surfaces that can be wet
  • if you have access to a sticker printer, use that
